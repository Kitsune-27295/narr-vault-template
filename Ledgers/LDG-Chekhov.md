# 🎯 Chekhov's Gun Ledger (Plants & Payoffs)

> Invariant: Every narrative element, weapon, technique hint, or political secret planted in the manuscript must be tracked to its dramatic payoff or explicit abandonment.
> 
> `Status` is strictly `planted`, `paid_off`, or `abandoned`.
> A payoff in the same chapter as the plant is a coincidence, not a payoff. Elements marked `abandoned` require an explanation row in [[Ledgers/LDG-Decisions|LDG-Decisions]].
