# ⚖️ Bible Lore Contradictions Ledger

> Formal, auditable register of identified source collisions, adaptation variants, translation lineage divergences, and chronological discrepancies across the story's universes.
> 
> **Epistemic Invariant:** Every conflict is recorded with explicit citations from both sides (Side A and Side B). Resolutions are authorial decisions documented with structural rationale so resolved disputes are never silently reopened or re-derived.

---

## 1. Governance & Arbitration Rules

1. **Dual Quotation Principle:** Both conflicting versions must be cited with their exact locators (file path, chapter, or databook reference).
2. **Prose Primacy:** In matters of drafted story prose versus provisional planning records, established manuscript prose holds authority.
3. **Point of Divergence Precedence:** When official canon and an AU Point of Divergence (`Bible/01_Divergences_AU/`) contradict each other, the AU POD governs unconditionally.
4. **Authorial Resolution Required:** AI agents never silently pick a winner; resolutions must be ratified and recorded with clear rationale.
