# 💡 Narrative Seeds & Concept Scratchpad

Inbox for uncommitted scene concepts, mechanical interactions, and speculative dialogue exchanges.

- **Seed 01:** What if the protagonist discovers that native energy channels resonate harmonically with relics from secondary cosmologies?
- **Seed 02:** A quiet campfire dialogue where the protagonist inadvertently references a foreign cultural concept, sparking curiosity in a native ally.
- **Seed 03:** A high-stakes tactical retreat where combining native mobility with foreign elemental shielding surprises an elite pursuer.
