# Rule 05: Engineering Excellence & Anti-Overengineering
1. **Dual Remediation Principle:** Whenever an error, bug, or continuity plot hole is detected, you MUST fix both the immediate symptom and the structural root cause.
2. **KISS & Anti-Overengineering:** Pursue the cleanest, most direct, and maintainable solution. Never introduce hypercomplex abstractions or bureaucratic overhead.
3. **Continuous Hygiene:** Evaluate and eliminate obsolete, redundant, or orphaned files and code.
4. **Hemingway's Iceberg Ratio (10/90):** Detail only the 10% of worldbuilding that directly supports the active and upcoming 3 chapters. The remaining 90% stays in lightweight ideation notes in `06_Ideation_and_Unblocking/`.
