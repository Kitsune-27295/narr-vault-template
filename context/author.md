# Authorial Voice & Narrative Direction

> Fill this note in at project start. Agents read it second in the cold-start sequence, so keep it short, concrete, and decisive.

## 1. Tone and Atmosphere
- **Register:** (grounded, lyrical, ironic, ...) and what to avoid.
- **Pacing:** how action, dialogue, and reflection are balanced; what a typical scene beat looks like.
- **Emotional authenticity:** what drives character behavior in this world.

## 2. Perspective & Character Voice
- **Primary POV:** (for example third-person limited anchored on one character).
- **Protagonist demeanor:** how they speak, decide, and conceal.
- **Secondary voices:** one line per recurring character; link to their Bible profile.

## 3. Power Systems & Mechanics Invariants
- State the non-negotiable rules of every power system, their costs, and their limits.
- Record how foreign power systems convert (or fail to convert) when universes cross; link the governing `PWR_*` note.

## 4. Relationships & Themes
- How relationships progress and what the story refuses to do.
