# Author Voice and Prose Contract

> **Prose Specification Contract.** Defines the stylistic guidelines, sentence architecture, paragraph cadence, and dialogue registers for manuscript drafting and translation.

## Fixed format

- **Draft language:** Natural Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`). Publication language: Spanish (`es`), 1:1 paragraph parity.
- **Paragraph markers:** Every paragraph starts with `<!-- p:N -->`.
- **Dialogue and thought:** Dialogue formatted in double quotes `""`; internal thoughts formatted in single quotes `''`.
- **Chapter header block:** Canonical header block placed directly below the chapter title.
- **Length budget:** Approximately 4,500 words per chapter (`vault.yaml`).

## Narration

- **Point of view:** Close third person focusing on one perspective character per chapter or scene.
- **Tense:** Past tense.
- **Paragraph size:** Average ~55-65 words per paragraph; one sensory beat or thematic unit per paragraph.
- **Sentence size:** Average ~20-25 words. In high-stakes combat or peril, sentences shorten for kinetic rhythm.
- **Sensory grounding:** Ground scenes in concrete physical objects, lighting, fabric, temperature, and acoustics before initiating motion.

## Dialogue

- **Register:** Distinct register bands: deferential courtesy to masters/elders, egalitarian parity to peers, cold/terse under lethal pressure.
- **Action pairing:** Pair dialogue tags with tactile micro-actions rather than empty speech adverbs.
- **Pacing:** Avoid long expository soliloquies in active scenes.

## Tone & Axioms

- **Atmosphere:** Earned progression, tangible physical consequences, and psychological consistency.
- **Forbidden:** Modern slang, breaking the fourth wall, meta-commentary, unearned power-ups, cartoonish character motivations.
- **Power on the page:** Every supernatural ability displayed must demonstrate its sensory feedback, energetic cost, or hard limitation in the same scene.
