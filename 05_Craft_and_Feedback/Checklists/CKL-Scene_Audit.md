# CKL-Scene_Audit — Scene Audit Checklist

Use with the `scene-audit` skill. Sources are credited in [[CKL-Craft_Sources]]. The wording is the framework's own.

## The five commandments
- [ ] **Inciting incident:** an event upsets the opening balance, and someone's choice or chance caused it.
- [ ] **Turning point:** the value at stake flips polarity.
- [ ] **Crisis:** the character faces a real choice, a best bad choice or two goods that exclude each other.
- [ ] **Climax:** the character acts on the crisis, on the page.
- [ ] **Resolution:** the new state differs from the opening state, and the cost is visible.

## Scene or sequel
- [ ] A scene names a goal, opposition and an outcome (yes, no, yes-but, no-and).
- [ ] A sequel names a reaction, a dilemma and a decision that sets the next goal.
- [ ] The chapter alternates the two without long runs of either.

## Failure modes
- [ ] No change in the value at stake.
- [ ] The crisis is decided for the character.
- [ ] The climax is explained, not dramatised.
- [ ] The resolution is missing.
- [ ] Several turning points compete.
- [ ] The scene only delivers information.

## One-sentence test
Write: who wants what, what stops them, what changes. If the sentence needs two "and"s, the scene may hold two scenes.
