# Pitch & Tone Bible

## Narrative Pitch & Core Atmosphere
Cinematic tone, emotional resonance, and world texture (gritty yet heroic). Focus on earned growth, tangible combat consequences, and deep psychological realism.

## Thematic Negative Boundaries: What NEVER Happens
- No unearned power-ups without physical, spiritual, or emotional cost.
- Antagonists never act purely out of cartoonish malice; all have coherent self-justified motives.
- Modern slang, anachronistic humor, and meta-commentary are strictly forbidden.
- The laws of physics and energy costs declared in [[SYS-Power_System]] are immutable; plot convenience never overrides metaphysical limits.
- A power that exceeds the canon baseline is labeled "Fanfic divergence" and listed in the point-of-divergence register. Its cost is either stated or marked "Cost: UNRESOLVED (curator decision)".
- A chapter may not use a power whose cost is UNRESOLVED until the curator sets it.
