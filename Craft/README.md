# 🎨 Craft & Feedback Layer

> Dedicated domain for narrative architecture audits, character voice guides, scene diagnostics, Story Grid surgical reviews, and beta reader simulations.

---

## 1. Directory Structure

| UID | Directory / Component | Category | Status | Summary / Scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `CRT-GUIDES` | [[Craft/Guides/Style_And_Pacing_Guide\|Guides/]] | Style & Pacing | active | Stylistic constraints, sensory texture guidelines, POV rules, and dialogue registers. |
| `CRT-CRITIQUES` | `Critiques/` | Diagnostics | active | In-depth chapter critiques generated by `$revision-pass` and specialized review passes. |
| `CRT-AUDITS` | `Audits/` | Structural Audits | active | Structural diagnostics from `$narrative-architecture-audit`, `$tone-and-voice-audit`, and `$foreshadowing-audit`. |

---

## 2. Inviolable Rule: Protection of Manuscript

Feedback, audits, and suggested scene improvements belong here in `Craft/` or as proposed diffs. Never rewrite author prose in `Manuscript/` in place without explicit instruction.
