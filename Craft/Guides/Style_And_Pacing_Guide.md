# 📖 Prose Style & Pacing Diagnostic Guide

## 1. Five Commandments of Story Grid Scene Design
Every scene in `Manuscript/pt-BR/` must fulfill the 5 Story Grid structural units:
1. **Inciting Incident:** An event breaks the protagonist's temporary status quo (causal or coincidental).
2. **Progressive Complications:** Escalating obstacles and active conflicts raise stakes and force active tactical adjustments.
3. **Crisis:** A genuine, dilemma-driven choice between two best goods or two lesser evils.
4. **Climax:** The protagonist takes definitive, irrevocable action executing their crisis choice.
5. **Resolution:** A new micro-state emerges; emotional and physical consequences settle.

## 2. Sensory Texture & Anti-AI Fluff Rules
- **Concrete Physical Anchors:** Ground scenes in weight, breath temperature, muscle tremor, scent of iron/ozone, and shoe traction against damp soil.
- **No Promotional Fluff:** Eliminate hyperbolic descriptions ("unprecedented majesty", "the world held its breath", "an impossible prodigy"). Show mechanical competence through observation and reaction.
- **Active Dialogue Registers:** Keep dialogue rhythm sharp and clipped during combat. Save philosophical depth for quiet post-crisis campfire aftermaths.
