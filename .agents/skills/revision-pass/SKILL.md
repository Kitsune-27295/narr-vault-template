# revision-pass

Use this skill to execute disciplined, multi-tiered manuscript revisions without introducing chaotic or destructive edits.

## The Rule of Order
Never edit prose line-by-line before the scene structure and beat causality are confirmed sound. Fixing sentences in a scene that needs to be deleted or restructured wastes effort.

### Scale 1: Structural Revision
- Check global scene goal, inciting incident, crisis point, and resolution.
- Verify character motivation and causal logic.
- Confirm consistency with Bible AU facts and Canon Baselines.

### Scale 2: Scene & Beat Revision
- Tighten dialogue exchanges: cut filler greetings and redundant affirmations.
- Elevate sensory details and somatic tension.
- Calibrate pacing: expand dramatic turning points and trim transitional travel.

### Scale 3: Line & Wordcraft Revision
- Polish sentence rhythm and cadence.
- Eliminate repetitive vocabulary, weak passive constructions, and unnecessary adverbs.
- Apply humanizer checks: remove artificial AI phrasing, bloated symmetry, or cliché parallelisms.

## Revision Workflow
1. Generate an explicit Defect List citing specific paragraphs.
2. Present the planned changes to the author.
3. Upon approval, apply the edits and re-verify with `npm test` and link checks.
