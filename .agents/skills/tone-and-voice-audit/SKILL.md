# tone-and-voice-audit

Use this skill to audit manuscript prose against the vault's established character voice guides and thematic guidelines.

## 1. Character Voice & Dialogue Audit
Compare spoken dialogue in drafts against `99_System/Lexicon_PT_ES/Character_Voice_Guide_ES.md` (and PT voice guides):
- **<Protagonist>:** measured, analytical, understated (replace with your voice guide).
- **<Deuteragonist>:** one line of register per recurring character (source: `context/author.md` and the Bible cast profile).

Flag lines where character dialogue sounds generic, interchangeable, or out-of-character.

## 2. Thematic Negative Boundaries
Check against forbidden tonal elements:
- No unearned modern slang or internet jargon in in-world dialogue.
- No passive protagonist apathy: the protagonist must remain motivated and proactive.
- No gratuitous edge: maintain grounded stakes and authentic emotional resonance.

## 3. Sensory Texture Audit
Ensure combat and emotional scenes feature somatic grounding:
- Muscle fatigue, energy flow warmth/strain, blade resonance, scent of ozone or scorched earth.
