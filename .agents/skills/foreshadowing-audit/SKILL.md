# foreshadowing-audit

Use this skill to track narrative setups, clues, abilities introduced, and their eventual payoffs across chapters.

## 1. Audit Clue Trajectories
Inspect the manuscript draft or scene for:
1. **Explicit Setups:** A weapon drawn, a secret technique glimpsed, a mysterious artifact pocketed, or a cryptic warning spoken.
2. **Implied Promises:** Romantic chemistry established, rivalry ignited, or looming political threats introduced.
3. **Payoff Deadlines:** Immediate (same chapter/scene), Short-term (same arc), or Long-term (future volume).

## 2. Check for Orphaned Promises
- Has a weapon or technique been introduced that vanishes when needed without explanation?
- Has a character's foreshadowed arrival failed to materialize?
- Are red herrings clearly resolved or left lingering as unintended confusion?

## 3. Audit Report
Format findings:
| Setup / Clue | Location Introduced | Expected Payoff Window | Current Status | Action Needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The sealed scroll | C01_S01 | Act I Academy Climax | Pending | Reinforce seal restriction |

Update scene audit notes in `Manuscript/pt-BR/` accordingly.
