# reader-sim

Use this skill to simulate the psychological experience of an engaged first-time fanfiction reader reviewing a draft.

## Procedure
Read the chapter sequentially from start to finish, tracking:
1. **Attention:** Where does the narrative grip the reader? Where does the eye want to skim?
2. **Curiosity:** What questions is the reader asking right now?
3. **Confusion:** Are spatial arrangements, power-system mechanics, or character movements unclear?
4. **Emotional Reward:** Does a turn, punchline, victory, or revelation feel satisfying?

## Output Structure
Present a paragraph-by-paragraph or beat-by-beat reaction timeline:
- **[p:01-05] Hook:** High interest; clear atmospheric grounding.
- **[p:06-12] Technical explanation:** Attention dips slightly during detailed power-system theory; recommend interspersing with physical interaction.
- **[p:13-20] Confrontation:** Spikes tension; satisfying payoff.

Provide actionable pacing recommendations without directly rewriting author prose.
