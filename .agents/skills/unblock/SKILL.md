# unblock

Use this skill when the author feels stuck, encounters narrative resistance, or loses momentum on a scene or chapter.

## 1. Two-Minute Diagnostic
Ask 1 to 3 targeted questions to identify the root cause of the block:
- **Option A — Information Gap:** "Do I lack a clear canon fact, power mechanic, or world detail?" $\rightarrow$ Route to `query` or `dossier-compile`.
- **Option B — Structural Uncertainty:** "Do I know where the scene needs to start and end, but not how to connect them?" $\rightarrow$ Route to `story-arc` or `narrative-architecture-audit`.
- **Option C — Character Resistance:** "Is the character doing something that feels false to their voice or established motivation?" $\rightarrow$ Route to `tone-and-voice-audit` or cast dossiers in `Bible/02_Cast_Characters/`.
- **Option D — Decision Paralysis:** "Am I stuck choosing between two equally viable plot directions?" $\rightarrow$ Route to `decide` or `conflict-arbitration`.

## 2. Immediate Next Action
Formulate the single smallest next physical action:
- Not: *"Write Chapter 2."*
- But: *"Draft the 3-line dialogue exchange where the mentor warns the protagonist about the barrier."*

Point to the exact skill that resolves the specific blocker and step aside.
