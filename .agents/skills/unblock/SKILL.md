# unblock

Use this skill when the work has stopped. It orients, diagnoses and routes. It writes no prose and creates no canon.

## Orient (two minutes)
1. Run `narr session brief` and read `memory/hot.md`, the active thread table and the current arc beat.
2. State in two lines where the story is and what the next chapter must achieve.

## Diagnose: ask, then pick one
Ask the curator at most three short questions, then name the most likely block:
- **Direction:** "I do not know what happens next." Route to `sandbox-diverge`.
- **Structure:** "I know what happens but it feels wrong." Route to `scene-audit` or `narrative-architecture-audit`.
- **Information:** "I do not know enough about X." Route to `lore-dossier`, `vault-query` or `deep-research`.
- **Voice:** "I cannot find the words." Suggest rereading `context/author_voice.md` and one earlier passage; route to `tone-and-voice-audit` only after a draft exists.
- **Energy:** "I am tired of this chapter." Suggest a smaller target: one scene, or one sequel, then stop.
- **Fear:** "I do not want to ruin it." Remind that drafts are cheap and every change is reversible through `revision-pass`.

## Next action
End with exactly one concrete, small next action that takes under ten minutes (open this file, write the goal of the next scene in one sentence, list three things that could go wrong). Never write the scene.

## Not this skill
Drafting belongs to `write-chapter`; deciding belongs to `narrative-decide`; recording lessons belongs to `session-wrapup`.
