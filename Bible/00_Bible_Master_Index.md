## Invariant Principles

1. **Precedence (AU over canon):** AU point-of-divergence notes (`Bible/01_Divergences_AU/`) take absolute precedence over canonical dossiers (`Canon/`).
2. **Pacing and value shifts:** each chapter advances an explicit Story Grid value shift and tracks emotional polarity changes.
3. **Language discipline:**
   - Framework configs, dossiers and Bible: **US English (`en-US`)**.
   - Drafting: **Portuguese (`pt-BR`)** in `Manuscript/pt-BR/`.
   - Publication: **Spanish (`es`)** in `Manuscript/es/`.
4. **Character agency:** every character, partner or rival has independent motives and a clear role; relationships develop through characterization, not shortcuts.
