# Narrative Arc 01: Awakening & Acclimation Template

## Core Narrative Mandate
- **Central Objective:** Ground the protagonist in the visceral physical reality of Universe Primary.
- **Primary Antagonistic Force:** Environmental hostility and internal cognitive shock.
- **Climax / First Plot Point:** Irrevocable decision committing the protagonist to an active stance within the world.

## Scene Progression
1. **Scene 01 (Hook):** Disorientation upon awakening; validation of living reality vs. fiction.
2. **Scene 02 (Investigation):** Confirming physical landmarks and testing personal energy flow.
3. **Scene 03 (Catalyst):** First external encounter with native scouts or patrol units.
