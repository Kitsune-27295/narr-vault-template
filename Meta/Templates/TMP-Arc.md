## 9. Standardized Lexicon & Taxonomy Glossary

| Term / Taxonomy | Category | Clear & Accessible Definition |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Narrative Arc (*ARC*)** | Structure | A complete multi-chapter dramatic sequence with unified stakes, beginning, middle, climax, and resolution. |
| **Dramatic Spine** | Storytelling | The central through-line driven by the protagonist's conscious Want and subconscious internal Need. |
| **Beat Sheet** | Structure | A chronological mapping of key narrative moments (Inciting Incident, Midpoint, Crisis, Climax) across chapters. |
| **Pinch Point** | Pacing | A focused scene reminding the audience and protagonist of the antagonist's relentless pressure and danger. |
| **Chekhov Gun** | Continuity | A dramatic detail, object, or promise introduced early that must produce a consequential narrative payoff. |
| **Sanderson Limitation** | Power | Hard constraint ensuring power gains are earned, measured, and balanced by exhaustion and material cost. |
