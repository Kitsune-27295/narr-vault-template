## 13. Operational Implementation & Lifecycle Management Protocols

### 13.1 Inception & Instantiation Protocol (Phase 1)
When creating a new character note:
1. **File Naming & Placement:** Always save under `02_Story_Bible/Characters/` with the exact filename `CHR-<Character_Name>.md` (zero spaces, Rule 10).
2. **Metadata Integrity:** Fill YAML frontmatter completely (`uid`, `type: character`, `title`, `tier_rank`, `primary_faction`, `tags`).
3. **Drafting Order:**
   - First, complete **Section 1 (Narrative Persona)** in 2–5 paragraphs to cement the character's living voice and presence.
   - Second, complete **Section 2 (Pre-Canon Backstory & Formative History)** in 2–5 paragraphs to establish their origin, awakening, and life context prior to Chapter 01.
   - Third, configure **Section 3 & 4 (Settei, Qizhi, and Voice)** to anchor visual and dialogue consistency.
   - Fourth, establish **Section 5 (Core Psychology)** detailing the Ghost, Lie, Want/Need, and Sanderson Sliders.
   - Fifth, fill **Section 6, 7 & 8 (Cultivation, Power Sources, and Relics)** according to their current story arc stage.
   - Sixth, establish **Section 10 & 11 (Relationships & Epistemic Horizon)** to guard against accidental authorial omniscience.
4. **Validation:** Run `python tools/character_manager.py audit` and `narr lint` to verify zero syntax errors.

### 13.2 Cultivation Breakthrough & Level-Up Protocol (Phase 2)
When a character advances in cultivation during manuscript progression:
1. **Summary & Frontmatter Sync:**
   - Update `tier_rank` in YAML frontmatter and the header decile rank in Section 6.1.
   - Rewrite the blockquote Executive Summary to reflect their new continental status and active combat tier.
2. **Ring / Bone Addition:**
   - Add the newly absorbed power source into the **Power Source Configuration table** in Section 7.1.
   - Specify the source beast, age, exact tactical effect, and explicit Sanderson limitations (drain/cooldown).
   - If an anatomical bone was acquired, add it to the corresponding row in Section 8.
3. **Biological / Mental Recalculation:**
   - Check if physical endurance or spiritual power realm tier shifted (e.g. Tier 2 -> Tier 3).
   - If Rank 70 is reached, ensure the **Signature Transformation** row in Section 7.1 is activated.
   - If Rank 80/90 is reached, record any newly condensed **Power Core** in Section 6.3.
4. **Timeline Append:**
   - Add a single dated line at the bottom of **Section 12 (Chronological Timeline Log)** linking to the chapter where the breakthrough occurred (`- Year XX (Age YY): Absorbed [Ring/Bone] in <C##-Chapter>`).
   - **Never delete past timeline logs.**

### 13.3 Epistemic & Relational Audit Protocol (Phase 3)
When dramatic shifts occur in the story:
1. **Relational Matrix:** If alliances fracture or trust deepens, update the `Trust Level` and `Dynamic Tension` column in Section 10.
2. **Epistemic Horizon:** If a secret is uncovered or a blindspot is resolved, remove it from `Critical Blindspots` and record it under `Secrets Known` in Section 11.
3. **Ledger Synchronization:** Run `python tools/character_manager.py sync-ledger` to update `08_Ledgers/LDG-Character_Matrix.md`.
